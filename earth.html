Earth, the "Blue Marble" and the third planet from the Sun, is an exceptional world uniquely positioned within the habitable zone to sustain vast 
            oceans of liquid water, fostering a dynamic biosphere made possible by its insulating atmosphere, protective magnetic field, 
            and continuous plate tectonics.

🔍

Overview

Earth is the third planet from the Sun and the only known planet to harbor life. It's the largest of the terrestrial planets and the fifth-largest planet in our solar system.

Planet TypeTerrestrial (Rocky)
Position3rd from the Sun
Age4.543 billion years
Named AfterOld English and Germanic words for "ground"
📏

Dimensions & Size

Earth is the largest of the rocky planets in our solar system. About 71% of Earth's surface is covered by water, giving it its distinctive blue appearance from space.

Diameter12,742 km (7,918 mi)
Equatorial Circumference40,075 km
Volume1,083,206,916,846 km3
Surface Area510.1 million km2
Land Area148.9 million km2 (29.2%)
⚖

Mass & Gravity

Earth is the densest planet in our solar system. Its iron-nickel core generates a strong magnetic field that protects the planet from harmful solar radiation and cosmic rays.

Mass 5.972 x 1024 kg
Escape Velocity11.19 km/s
Density 5.514 g/cm³ (highest in solar system)
Surface Gravity9.807 m/s² (1.0 g)
⛅

Atmosphere & Weather

Earth's atmosphere is unique in the solar system, containing the oxygen needed for complex life. It also protects us from harmful radiation and meteorites while maintaining a stable climate.

Composition90% Hydrogen, 10% Helium
Atmospheric PressureMethane, Ammonia, Water vapor
LayersAmmonia, Ammonium Hydrosulfide, Water
Weather SystemsUp to 640 km/h
👫

Life & Biosphere

Earth is the only known planet with life. From microscopic bacteria to blue whales, life thrives in nearly every environment. The biosphere has fundamentally shaped Earth's atmosphere and geology.

Living Species~8.7 million estimated (1.2 million known)
First Life~3.8 billion years ago
Human Population~8 billion (as of 2024)
EcosystemsOceans, forests, deserts, tundra, etc.
🌡

Temperature

Jupiter radiates more energy than it receives from the Sun. The internal heat is generated by gravitational compression of the planet.

Highest Recorded56.7°C (134°F) - Death Valley
Lowest Recorded-89.2°C (-128.6°F) - Antarctica
Average Temperature15°C (59°F)
Ocean Average17°C (63°F)
🌐

Surface & Geology

Earth is a geologically active planet with plate tectonics constantly reshaping its surface. This activity recycles materials and helps maintain a stable climate over geological timescales.

Surface Composition71% water, 29% land
Tectonic Plates7 major, many minor plates
Highest PointMt. Everest - 8,849 m (29,032 ft)
Deepest PointMariana Trench - 10,994 m (36,070 ft)
🪃

Rotation & Orbit

Earth's 23.4-degree axial tilt gives us our seasons. We orbit the Sun at nearly 107,000 km/h, completing one orbit every 365.25 days. The extra quarter day is why we have leap years

Day Length23.93 hours (24 hours)
Year Length365.25 days
Distance from Sun149.6 million km (1 AU)
Orbital Speed29.78 km/s (107,000 km/h)
Axial Tilt23.44° (causes seasons)
🌙

Moons

The Moon is Earth's only natural satellite and the fifth-largest moon in the solar system. It creates ocean tides and helps stabilize Earth's axial tilt, making our climate more stable.

Total Moons1 moon (Luna)
Moon Diameter3,474 km (27% of Earth)
Distance384,400 km average
Effects on EarthTides, stabilizes axial tilt
FormationGiant impact ~4.5 billion years ago
🧲

Magnetic Field

Earth's magnetic field acts as a protective shield, deflecting harmful solar wind and cosmic radiation. Without it, the atmosphere would be slowly stripped away, making life impossible.

SourceLiquid iron-nickel outer core
Strength25-65 microteslas at surface
ProtectionShields from solar wind and cosmic rays
MagnetosphereExtends ~65,000 km on Sun-facing side
AurorasNorthern and Southern Lights
